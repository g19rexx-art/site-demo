Help Us Identify Problems in Bwaise

Residents can use this form to report environmental concerns affecting their neighbourhood. Reports may help improve communication between residents, service providers and relevant authorities.

Problems You Can Report

  • Illegal waste dumping
  • Overflowing rubbish collection points
  • Blocked drainage channels
  • Flooding
  • Other environmental concerns

Need Help Submitting a Report?

If you cannot take or upload a photograph, you may seek assistance from an authorised administrator or community representative who can help submit the report on your behalf.

Your contact details and uploaded photograph should only be accessible to authorised personnel. They should not be displayed publicly.

Environmental Report Form

Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required.

Provide at least one valid contact method: phone number or email address.

A photograph is required. Accepted formats: JPG, PNG and WEBP.

Before submitting: Check that your location and description are accurate and that your photograph clearly shows the reported problem.
