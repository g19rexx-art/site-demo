Environmental Education & Tips

Discover simple and practical ways to manage waste, protect drainage channels and maintain a healthier environment in Bwaise.

KNOWLEDGE FOR A CLEANER COMMUNITY

Small Actions Can Make a Big Difference

Proper waste management is important for protecting the environment and improving public health. When waste is carelessly disposed of, it can block drainage channels, create unpleasant surroundings and contribute to flooding.

Through environmental education, residents can learn responsible waste disposal practices and take part in keeping their neighbourhood clean and safe.

Protect Our Environment

A cleaner community begins with responsible choices made by individuals, households and businesses.

Practical Waste Management Tips

Follow these practices to help improve waste management in your household and community.

🗑️

Dispose of Waste Properly

Put household rubbish in appropriate waste containers and use recognised waste collection services. Avoid dumping rubbish on roadsides or in open spaces.

♻️

Separate Your Waste

Where suitable facilities exist, separate recyclable materials such as plastic, paper, glass and metal from other household waste.

🏠

Keep Your Surroundings Clean

Regularly clean your home, compound and nearby shared spaces. Store waste safely while waiting for collection.

🚯

Avoid Illegal Dumping

Do not throw rubbish into drainage channels, roads, wetlands or undeveloped plots. Use appropriate disposal arrangements.

Drainage Protection and Flood Awareness

Responsible waste disposal and early reporting can help communities respond to drainage and flooding concerns.

How Can Residents Help?

  • Never throw plastic bags or other rubbish into drainage channels.
  • Report blocked drains to the relevant authorities.
  • Avoid building or placing objects that obstruct water flow.
  • Pay attention to official weather and flood warnings.
  • Keep children away from dangerous floodwater and open drains.
  • Seek safe routes during heavy rainfall and flooding.

⚠️

Flood Safety Reminder

Floodwater may contain harmful waste and hidden hazards. Avoid walking or driving through floodwater whenever possible.

Follow guidance from relevant local authorities during flooding emergencies.

Environmental Hygiene and Public Health

Good hygiene practices help protect households and the wider community.

🧼

Wash Your Hands

Wash your hands with soap and clean water after handling waste and before preparing or eating food.

🪣

Store Waste Safely

Keep rubbish in suitable covered containers to reduce exposure to pests and unpleasant smells.

🧤

Handle Waste Carefully

Use suitable protective equipment when handling rubbish. Avoid touching sharp or hazardous waste directly.

🏥

Report Health Hazards

Report dangerous waste accumulation and other environmental concerns to the appropriate authorities.

Remember These Important Practices

✓ Use proper waste collection arrangements.

✓ Keep rubbish away from drainage channels.

✓ Participate in community environmental activities.

✓ Report environmental problems when you notice them.

✓ Share environmental knowledge with family and neighbours.
