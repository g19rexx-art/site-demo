OUR COMMUNITY, OUR RESPONSIBILITY

Everyone Has a Role to Play

Maintaining a clean and healthy environment requires the participation of everyone. Residents, community leaders, waste collection providers and relevant authorities can work together to reduce improper waste disposal and improve environmental awareness.

Bwaise Clean & Safe promotes community participation by providing information about environmental activities and encouraging residents to take responsibility for their surroundings.
