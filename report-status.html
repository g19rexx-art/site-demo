Understanding Report Status

Reports may move through the following stages as they are reviewed and handled.

1

Submitted

The report has been received by the platform.

2

Under Review

An authorised administrator is reviewing the report.

3

Referred

The report has been referred to a relevant service provider or authority.

4

Resolved

The reported issue has been addressed and the outcome recorded.
