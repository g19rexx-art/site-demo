WELCOME TO OUR PLATFORM

Why Proper Waste Management Matters

Bwaise Clean & Safe is a community-focused web platform designed to connect residents with waste collection providers, local authorities and community organisations.

The platform provides information about waste collection, supports the reporting of illegal dumping and blocked drainage channels, and encourages community participation in environmental improvement.
